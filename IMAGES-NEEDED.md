# Images Needed — Page by Page

**Priority key:** 🔴 Must-have · 🟠 High impact · 🟢 Nice-to-have · ✅ Already have

**General specs (all photos)**
- Real photos of your own crew, truck and jobs. Avoid stock or AI images; they hurt trust for a local trades business.
- Landscape, at least **1600px wide**, daylight. Save as `.webp` (under 300 KB is ideal).
- Show the branded truck or uniform where you can. No customer faces, house numbers or number plates.
- Before/after pairs: shoot both from the **same spot, same angle, same zoom**.
- Use descriptive, keyword-friendly file names (suggested names below) and put them in `public/images/`.
